An index of our own

Service is not a front end for someone else's search results. It runs its own crawler, builds its own index, and ranks pages with its own scoring code.

What we don't do

  • No tracking cookies, and no third-party analytics on this page.
  • No search profiles. Your queries are not attached to an identity.
  • No webfonts from a third-party CDN — the interface uses your system fonts.
  • Outbound result links carry rel="noreferrer", so sites you visit don't learn what you searched.

How ranking works

A page's score starts from how tightly your search terms cluster together in its text, normalised so that a very long document can't win simply by being long. That base relevance is then adjusted by a small, bounded set of signals: how many other indexed pages link to it, whether the query is essentially the site's own name, whether it's a homepage or a deep subpage, whether it declares itself canonical, and whether it has enough substance to be worth reading.

Two of those signals exist because of what this engine is for: pages served over plain HTTP are demoted, and URLs carrying campaign-tracking parameters are demoted in favour of the clean original.

Honest limitations

The index is small. A commercial engine has crawled a scale of the web that this project has not, so there will be queries Service simply cannot answer yet. Where it can compete is depth on the topics it has actually crawled — and on not treating the person searching as the product.

Sponsors

Service is kept running by its sponsors and by the ad slots on either side of the page. Those ads are contextless — they are not targeted using anything about you, because we don't collect anything to target with. Thank you to everyone chipping in.
