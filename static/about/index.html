About

A search engine built from scratch

Service crawls, ranks and serves the web itself. No syndicated results, no borrowed index, no ad-tech company deciding what you see.

What makes it different

Our own crawler
Every page is fetched and indexed by our crawler, which honours robots.txt. Nothing is licensed from Google or Bing.
No tracking
We don’t build a profile of you, and searches aren’t tied to an identity to be resold later.
Honest about scale
Our index is far smaller than Google’s. We publish the real number on the live counter rather than hiding it.
Ads, not surveillance
Two plain display ads keep the servers running. They’re not targeted using your search history, and you can dismiss them.

Thanks to our sponsors

Running a crawler and an index costs real money in bandwidth and storage. The ads in the side rails are what make an independent search engine possible without charging for access or selling search data. Thank you for leaving them on.
